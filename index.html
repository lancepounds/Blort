Beat sheet, as built

OpeningGumbo pops out of the toaster bus stop. Sign: "WELCOME. BREAKFAST ENDS WHEN APPROVED." Only route is right.
TeachingGate with a whistle plaque. Whistle near it; it salutes and opens.
DistractionA Deputy Spoon patrols past the gate. If it sees you it shouts HALT! and throws a citation. Whistle and it walks off to inspect the nearest cone, sign or egg, and you can walk right past.
EnemyApology Crab on a narrow platform. "sorry!" is the charge telegraph. Stomp it, or whistle to stun.
PuzzlePick up the Official Stamp. It has two inks: APPROVED makes things do more, FORBIDDEN makes them stop. Approve a PENDING platform to reach a Form, approve the UNTOASTED bridge to cross, then forbid an "officially approved" barrier to get past it.
EscalationAnnouncement makes all bridges illegal. The bridge is unaffected.
Mini-bossDeputy Spoon Prime throws citations. Whistle at a filing cabinet to return them. Three returns win.
1-2 StretchPick up Sock Stretch. Press S near a handle or a gap and Gumbo reaches across by himself.
1-2 BossesBaron von Lint (tug his tag with Stretch), Chairman Waffle (throw pocket ducks), General Crumb (whistle for a salute, then stamp FORBIDDEN), Form 1040-Ω (tug, duck and stamp its three boxes).
1-2 PocketEmergency Pocket holds one item from a kiosk: a key for the Annex door, Helium Toast to float to a high ledge, or Rubber Ducks that home in on bosses.
Exit gagGiant END OF LEVEL door stays shut. A tiny side door opens. The giant door gets the applause.

Slice success checks

  • First objective readable without text: the level only scrolls right.
  • Gameplay jokes: the saluting gate, the apology telegraph, the unaffected illegal bridge, citations filed back at their author, the applauded door.
  • Fair controls: coyote time, jump buffering, variable jump height.
  • One stamp rule everywhere: approved crabs charge harder, forbidden crabs become furniture you can stand on, forbidden citations are void, approved ones fly faster.
  • The whistle always tells you something: eggs salute and give a tip, signs flip over to show a hint, cones hop and reveal hidden Yawns.
  • Fast failure: death message, then respawn at the last Approved Breakfast Station in under a second.
  • Collectibles: Yawns, Forms, a Loose Button, and one hidden Certified Nothing.

Hunt tip: the welcome sign is load-bearing.
